# Program principles and references

Reviewed for this release: 3 November 2026. The 30 programs are original coaching templates, not individually validated trials or medical treatment. Exact exercise order, set allocation, deload dates and load increments are implementation choices.

## How the app uses evidence

- Progressive overload is included in every program. Strength work emphasizes heavier rep ranges and longer rest; beginner stages use manageable loads and technique reserve.
- Hypertrophy focus muscles generally receive about 10–20 primary working sets across at least two sessions. Supporting areas also receive compound work; introductory weeks and deloads intentionally reduce volume.
- Training twice weekly is a practical way to distribute volume. The frequency review does not establish a universal advantage for a particular schedule when weekly volume is equal.
- Core hypertrophy and fat loss are different outcomes. The two ab programs explicitly reject spot reduction; nutrition and overall energy balance drive body-fat loss.
- Band training is a practical resistance option. The elastic-resistance review addresses strength comparisons and does not validate this exact band routine or prove every hypertrophy response equivalent.
- Mobility and core-control blocks use comfortable ranges and modest fatigue. Their names do not promise a permanent posture correction or cure for pain.
- Power training uses low reps, controlled landings and full recovery rather than fatigue circuits. Advanced readiness is required.
- Nutrition notes are general references for healthy exercising adults. No starting weights, calorie target or predicted weight loss are calculated from gender or body weight.

## Recommendation rules

Required equipment and location must match first. Long-band and low-bar setup checks prevent assuming that a mini-loop or a high doorway bar can perform those full programs. Recommendations also respect experience and maximum available days; they favor a general plan before muscle specializations when a suitable general option exists. Custom plans are user-designed.

## Combined workload estimates

Warnings inspect actual scheduled sessions after introductory stages, ability stages, deloads and weekday shifts. Primary hard sets count once and indirect sets as half; unilateral sets are counted per muscle rather than doubled. This half-set rule is a planning heuristic, not a precise biological equivalence. Warm-up, mobility and conditioning do not inflate hard-set totals. Consecutive exposure includes conditioning and the following Monday after Sunday; gentle mobility is excluded. A week with no day free of scheduled sessions gets a rest-day warning.

## Video behavior

Videos are demonstrations rather than evidence that a routine produces a particular outcome. Existing source metadata remains in VIDEO_SOURCES.md. New IDs stay blank until selected. The app uses the existing privacy-enhanced YouTube embed and the official IFrame API for failure events. Error 100 flags an unavailable/private video, 101/150 flag embed restrictions, and network/configuration failures remain unconfirmed. Written instructions are always available.
