## Life-stage sections

Each dedicated section has ten plans. Pregnancy options use conservative upright or supported activity and moderate effort. Postpartum options separate gentle recovery from rebuilding strength, with readiness based on healing and symptoms. The 65+ section includes seated, standing, supported-balance, gym and home-equipment options; floor transfers are explicitly optional.

These are original example templates informed by guideline principles. The exact combinations are not clinically validated interventions, personalized treatment or a replacement for clinical screening. The maternal suitability acknowledgement and postpartum readiness gate determine recommendations and activation. General profiles never receive maternal plans automatically. Pregnancy and postpartum activation uses one active primary plan; earlier bookmarks and logs remain available.

The full guidance, stop symptoms and source links appear in profile setup, the relevant catalog section and each relevant plan preview. Maternal plans are not prescribed to reach a muscle-growth volume target. Starting activity doses may be below population weekly targets.

## Evidence and interpretation

The sources support programming principles and population guidance; they do not endorse these exact 195 app templates. Progression is conservative, and postpartum progression is symptom-led rather than calendar-led. Sets in muscle-specialization programs total about 10–20 primary sets for the named muscle section in a normal week; warm-ups do not count. Other muscles receive supporting work rather than a claim that every program maximizes all muscles.

Primary sources reviewed on 4 November 2026:

- [ACSM 2026 resistance-training position stand](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12965823/). Resistance programming, progressive overload and flexibility in implementation.
- [CSEP/SOGC pregnancy guideline](https://csepguidelines.ca/guidelines/pregnancy/) and [ACOG pregnancy guidance](https://www.acog.org/womens-health/faqs/exercise-during-pregnancy). Eligibility, activity types, modification and symptom awareness.
- [CSEP 2025 postpartum guideline](https://csepguidelines.ca/guidelines/postpartum/) and [screening questionnaire](https://csep.ca/2025/03/21/csep-get-active-questionnaire-for-postpartum/). Early gentle movement, sufficient healing and individualized progression toward a 120-minute weekly target over four or more days.
- [CDC older-adult recommendations](https://www.cdc.gov/physical-activity-basics/guidelines/older-adults.html), [NSCA older-adult resistance-training statement](https://www.nsca.com/about-us/position-statements/resistance-training-for-older-adults/) and [NHS sitting exercises](https://www.nhs.uk/live-well/exercise/sitting-exercises/). Strength, aerobic activity, balance and ability-matched starting points.
- [WHO physical activity recommendations](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/physical-activity). General aerobic activity targets.
