# Equipment artwork

Six original equipment covers were generated with the built-in image-generation tool for this app, reviewed, and saved as optimized local JPEG assets (960 × 640). No external image service, tracking request or remote image license is required for display. They are illustrations of equipment rather than technique demonstrations or additional equipment requirements.

Shared prompt: “Premium fitness-app equipment photography; realistic editorial still life; functional equipment geometry, detailed materials, soft directional lighting, generous negative space; graphite, warm gray and subtle lime palette; landscape 3:2; no people, text, branding or watermark.”

Subjects:

- Dumbbells: black hex dumbbells on graphite rubber flooring, towel and unbranded bottle.
- Barbell: Olympic barbell, rubber plates and adjustable bench.
- Bands: long resistance bands, rolled mat and sturdy armless chair in a home setting.
- Kettlebell: charcoal kettlebell beside a folded towel.
- Outdoor: running shoes, park path, low steps and a sturdy training rail.
- Mobility: exercise mat, foam blocks and a sturdy armless chair.

The original generated images are retained in the authoring workspace; the app uses only these local optimized files. The shared prompt and subject list above document the artwork brief. These covers are reused according to each program’s setting and equipment.
